Why we meet

Community & culture

A regular space to meet one another and enjoy films collectively, building relationships and a culture of discussion outside formal political spaces.

Political discussion

Different interpretations, disagreements and political questions are part of the discussion. Talking them through can help us sharpen our ideas and learn from one another.

A wider audience

Films people want to see, affordable tickets and a welcoming atmosphere can bring people together whether or not they are already politically involved.
