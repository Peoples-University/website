The People’s University Project is an organization which aims to **democratically intervene** in the political activities of the masses and other progressive groups in the greater Vancouver area in order to r**aise their political and class consciousness**, **promote working class self-activity** toward building revolutionary mass organizations, and produce **ideological theory** that responds to contemporary problems.

These interventions are based on s**cientific study of political economy** and can take the form of

* **public position papers** investigating, analyzing, and contributing to theoretical understanding of problems manifesting in movement spaces and broader society,
* **political and ideological education** to support the development of organizers, workers and rank-and-file members engaged in building mass organizations, 
* **popular education and events** to raise the political and class consciousness of the masses, and/or
* **participation in organizations** to strengthen and build their mass character and raise their revolutionary activity. 



# Why science?

We aim for our method to be a scientific one! But what is science? Well, we would give a definition which affirms that:

1. Science is an **ideological project** borne of the struggle between society and nature and the attendant necessity of developing the productive forces of society. 
2. The object of a science is a form of ***matter*** in ***motion***. 
3. The task of a science is to move from the ***appearance*** of its object (i.e., its observable features, quantitative and qualitative) to the ***essence*** of its object (i.e., its essential characteristics and relations, its laws of motion).
4. The method for producing a scientific theory is first to ***investigate*** in order to document the *appearance* of the object of study, i.e., to establish a set of ***empirical facts*** that describe it; and then, on the basis of this, to ***analyze*** in order to uncover the *essence* of the object of study, i.e., to develop ***rational arguments*** that describe its essential nature and laws of motion.
5. The procedure for scientific analysis is first one of ***deconstructive abstraction*** and its result is the simplest abstract conceptions that form the basis of a scientific theory; this is followed by a process of ***reconstructive concretization*** and its result is a complex, concrete conception of the object of study.
6. The correctness of a scientific theory is determined in **social *practice***, i.e., peer-reviewed experiment, social production, and class struggle. A correct scientific theory constitutes **scientific knowledge.** 
7. The material form of scientific knowledge is **information**. The ideological content of scientific knowledge is the rigorous **proof** of the correctness of scientific theories.  
8. The progress of scientific knowledge is the result of **a struggle between theories that are, in relative terms, correct and incorrect**. This struggle unfolds and expresses itself in the process of the ***exposition*** and ***critique*** of scientific theories but, in the end, is determined in the crucible of social practice. 



Investigation and analysis, then, are key components of the People’s University’s method. In particular, our object of study, society, requires that we conduct **social investigation and class analysis (SICA)** as well as **economic investigation and politico-economic analysis**. These forms of investigation and analysis are the most important aspect of the committee’s work. 


Furthermore, we aim for our method to be one that centers genuine dialogue and struggle. We understand that struggling against the capitalist class requires unity and that our internal unity is itself built by struggling with one another, by engaging in genuine dialogue. Therefore, in order to resolve the disagreements (or even social conflicts) that emerge within the cohort, we intend to approach them through the framework **unity-struggle-unity**. That is, we intend to approach disunity by returning to our already established principles of *unity*, by *struggling* over the question at hand (be it ideological, political, organizational, interpersonal, or otherwise), and *uniting* on a deeper level based on what we learn by struggling over things and facilitating dialogue. Moreover, one of the primary frameworks we intend to employ in navigating disagreement among members is that of **constructive criticism and self-criticism**. This means distinguishing between objective observations (i.e. descriptions of what people have said or done) and subjective feelings interpretations; it means identifying what people should say or do instead; and it means explaining the purpose for this recommendation.

# 
Style of work

* In carrying out the work of the committee, we aim to be **principled** while also being **non-sectarian**. Humility and comradeliness are key ingredients here!
* The durability of our work requires a division that is **equitable**, a division that is based on the ability and capacity of the committee’s membership. Our division of work also bears on our ability to build **democracy and centralism** in the organization, key features of a durable unity. For these reasons, decisions about our division of work, especially those related to its *expansion (quantitative)* and *evolution (qualitative),* should be approached with **care** for the committee’s membership as well as a long-range, **strategic awareness**.
* Our work must stay grounded in **a love for, faith in, and trust of the broad masses** of exploited and oppressed people. They are the makers of history! This fact is the basis for any real hope in a better future for humanity, and this **hope must be a discipline** for leaders of the people. Our work should inspire hope for a better world!
* Interpersonal harm is an inevitable part of being in community, and we’re committed to responding to it in ways that promote real **healing, accountability, and growth**.
* The compass that guides our work is the interests and welfare of the broad masses of exploited and oppressed people, especially those in the city we share. **Local working-class struggles are struggles of the working class the world over.**



# Membership

General members of the People’s University Project(PUP) are members that are interested in the work of PuP but want to support the project at a more casual level. General members:

1. Have a **stated interest in making a scientific analysis of society**. 
2. If a general member is interested in independently writing, publishing, or publicly studying things related to the Points of Unity of PUP, they are **expected to setup a way to collaborate with the People’s University Committee (PUC)** through:
3. * Submitting the work to PUC for review and feedback

   * Collaborating with PUC in hosting events
4. Are **interested in providing feedback** on the materials produced by PUC. Prior to publishing materials or hosting events, PUC will host meetings to get feedback on slides & papers.
5. **Interested in attending PUP events**. Expected to attend 2-3 events of PUP per year OR one course hosted by PUP per year.
6. * This can include the courses done by PUC, such as Organizer School, Code against the Machine & course Political Economy
7. Can **make proposals** to PUP
8. Can **request to attend PUP meetings**
9. Are encouraged to join working groups focused on **position papers** where they can contribute insights, offer critical feedback, or assume responsibility for **scientific investigation** and the drafting of specific sections.
10. Can **access materials produced by PUC if requested**.
11. Are **encouraged to write and share reports** about their experiences in political organizations and comment on the political, organizational, and ideological character of said experiences
