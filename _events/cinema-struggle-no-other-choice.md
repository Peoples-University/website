No Other Choice is a 2025 South Korean black comedy thriller film co-written, produced, and directed by Park Chan-wook.  The film follows a desperate paper industry expert who decides to kill off his competition to be assured of the job he seeks to maintain his way of life.

We will host a lively debate and discussion about the political themes of the film after the screening. We hope to see you there! Tickets are $6 until July 1, and can be bought using the QR code or link in our bio
