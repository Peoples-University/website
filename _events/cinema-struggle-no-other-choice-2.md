summary: No Other Choice is a 2025 South Korean black comedy thriller film
  co-written and directed by master filmmaker Park Chan-wook. Based on the 1997
  crime novel The Ax by Donald E. Westlake, the movie serves as a biting,
  anti-capitalist satire of corporate greed, unemployment, and economic anxiety.
  The story follows You Man-su (played by Squid Game star Lee Byung-hun), a
  devoted family man who enjoys an idyllic, comfortable middle-class life with
  his wife, two children, and two dogs. His life is upended when he is
  unceremoniously laid off from his 25-year career as a middle manager at a pulp
  manufacturer after the company is taken over.
