We're hosting our first General Membership meeting! This is an opportunity for folks to share updates from organizing they've been doing around the city, hear updates from us & make proposals for the People's University Project! Our goal for this meeting is to create a space where people feel comfortable sharing issues they've been facing & contributing positively by making constructive criticisms of other organizing work!

## Agenda

1. Reportbacks from organizations around the city
2. Updates from PuP on research & writing
3. Proposals for the People's University Project
