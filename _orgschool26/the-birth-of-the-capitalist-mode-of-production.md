summary: >-
  In this session, we’ll begin to discuss political economy - what some might
  call the science of commodity production. This week is all about **the
  historical processes that were necessary in order for capitalism to come into
  existence**. With this in mind, we’ll explore the relationship between the
  commercial trade of enslaved Africans, the genocide of Indigenous peoples in
  the Americas, the witch-hunting of women, and the expropriation of peasants.
  And most importantly, we’ll talk about why each of these historical processes
  was so crucial to the emergence of capitalism.
