In particular, we’ll discuss the nuances of the mass line method of political leadership (i.e., a method of formulating demands that essentially moves “from the masses, to the masses”). We’ll also talk about the importance of social investigation and class analysis (or SICA) in demand identification and the formulation of a mass line. We’ll explore the relationship between unity and struggle, the difference between political and interpersonal conflicts, and practices for resolving either in a way that strengthens our unity."
