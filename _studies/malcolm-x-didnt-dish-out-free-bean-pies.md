Join us at the SFPIRG for a political education session on the revolutionary organization! **We’re going to be learning about the history of the different forms and models of political organization and discussing how these different forms take shape in today’s political environment**. In particular, we’ll be looking at an article by Kenny Lake titled, “Malcolm X Didn’t Dish Out Free Bean Pies” and learning about the nature of the mutual aid practices of Black Panther Party.
