💰⛓️‍💥Down with Imperialism 🗣️💥 **During this session we’re going to be discussing a book written by V.I. Lenin titled, Imperialism, the Highest Stage of Capitalism.** Themes for this discussion will include the political economy of imperialism (i.e., monopoly capitalism), the nature of inter-imperialist rivalry and competition, the relationship between imperialism and the “labour aristocracy,” and the strategy for the defeat of imperialism.
